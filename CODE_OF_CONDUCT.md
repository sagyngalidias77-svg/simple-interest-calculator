# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Use respectful language, welcome constructive feedback, and consider the needs of other contributors. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify these standards and address reports fairly. They may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using their GitHub profile contact options. Maintainers will review reports and protect the privacy of the people involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the conduct and its impact, maintainers may issue a correction or warning, or impose a temporary or permanent ban. They will explain their decision to the affected contributor when appropriate.
